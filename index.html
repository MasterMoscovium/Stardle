Welcome to Stardle!

The astronomy word game by Dhruva Astronomy Club

Stardle works just like the classic Wordle — but every word is an astrophysics term. Click the Hint button for a musical hint that leads you toward the word. A new word drops every day at midnight IST.

How to Play

Guess the Stardle in 6 tries.

  • Each guess must be a valid 5-letter word.
  • The color of the tiles will change to show how close your guess was.

Examples

ORBIT

O is in the word and in the correct spot.

STARS

T is in the word but in the wrong spot.

NOVAE

A is not in the word in any spot.

A new puzzle is released every day at midnight IST. Built on Wordle-Vanilla by doeixd.
